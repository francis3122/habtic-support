Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Habtic is a private habit and self-motivation app. This policy explains how Habtic handles information.

Data stored on your device

Your goals, internal balances, ledger, products, vouchers, installment plans, task completion records, settings, and photos you choose are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. Habtic does not require an account.

Data collection and sharing

Habtic does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data. It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.

Public content downloads

Habtic may download public task and quote packages from a configured content server. These requests retrieve the same public files for all users and do not upload your goals, ledger, photos, task records, or a user identifier.

Photos and notifications

Photos are accessed only when you explicitly select one to record a completion process or decorate a personal reward. Notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device and can be disabled in Habtic or System Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it. In Habtic, open Profile → Privacy and data → Erase all local data. Removing the app also removes its local data. Habtic has no server-side account or personal profile to retain.
